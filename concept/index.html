How the game works

One partner creates a private couple space and shares a short invitation code. The other partner joins, and both can take part in activities together. Yarper is designed around simple, repeatable moments of play rather than complicated controls or long sessions.

  • Join together: create or enter a private two-person room using an invitation code.
  • Choose an activity: explore questions, challenges, Truth or Dare, conversation cards and date-night prompts.
  • Take part: respond to prompts, share answers and react during a synchronized session.
  • Build progress: earn experience points, progress through levels and engage with daily quests and streak rewards.

Core features

  • Private two-person rooms and invitation codes.
  • Interactive questions and conversation prompts.
  • Light-hearted challenges and Truth or Dare.
  • Date-night ideas and themed cards.
  • Shared play sessions, partner responses and emoji reactions.
  • XP, levels, daily quests and streak-based rewards.
  • Responsive interface designed for phones and desktop browsers.

Audience and experience

Yarper is intended for couples looking for an easy, playful way to spend time together, spark conversations and discover activities they can enjoy as a pair. The experience aims to be welcoming, simple to start and suitable for short sessions.
